Project datasets
================

1. Vasiliev Sagittarius Catalog
Source:
https://vizier.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/VizieR?-source=J/MNRAS/501/2279

The catalog is retrieved by src/reading_catalog_ps4.ipynb through
astroquery.vizier. Generated local copies are stored in data/ as
vasiliev_sgr.fits and vasiliev_sgr.csv.

2. Gaia DR3 XP Continuous Spectra
Source:
https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/

Gaia DR3 source IDs are obtained by matching the Gaia DR2 identifiers
from the Vasiliev catalog through gaiadr3.dr2_neighbourhood. XP
continuous coefficients are downloaded with astroquery.gaia and stored
in data/Vasiliev_Sgr_XP_batches/.

Run src/reading_catalog_ps4.ipynb from top to bottom to recreate these
files. Downloads are resumable.

3. APOGEE DR17 allStar Catalog
Source:
https://www.sdss4.org/dr17/irspec/spectro_data/

The APOGEE DR17 allStar catalog supplies the stellar labels used for
model training. The full catalog is not stored in this repository
because of its size. The training pipeline should download or read the
catalog, apply the documented quality cuts, and crossmatch the selected
stars with Gaia DR3 XP continuous spectra.
